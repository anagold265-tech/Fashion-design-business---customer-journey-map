# Fashion-design-business---customer-journey-map
A customer journey map for a local fashion design business strategy , detailing the Awareness, Consideration, Conversion, Loyalty and Advocacy 
 Mapping out a customer journey for a local business of your choice.
    Mapping out a customer journey for a local custom fashion design business.
1. Awareness:
Discovers the brand while looking for custom outfit designs for an upcoming event through Instagram Reels, TikTok videos, local Google Maps listing, word-of-mouth recommendations.  Post high-quality showcase videos of completed outfits, client transformations, and behind-the-scenes tailoring. 
 2. Consideration:
 Evaluates the designer's style, craftsmanship, turnaround time, and pricing transparency. Instagram Direct Messages (DM), WhatsApp Business catalog, website portfolio, online customer reviews. Send a clear price guide, fabric options chart, consultation booking link, and social proof (testimonials). 
3. Conversion:
Commits to ordering a custom dress/suit, agrees on specs, and pays the initial deposit.  In-person boutique consultation, design sketching session, body measurement form, invoice/payment link. Conduct a detailed consultation, sketch initial concepts, provide fabric swatches, and issue a formal order receipt.
4. Loyalty:
  Experiences the creation process, receives a perfectly fitted garment, and returns for future needs. Mid-project fitting sessions, progress update messages (WhatsApp/SMS), final pickup packaging, care instructions. Send behind-the-scenes progress photos, host a final fitting to ensure fit accuracy, and include a styled care guide in final packaging. 
5. Advocacy:
 Actively recommends the fashion brand to friends, family, and online followers.  Instagram story/post tags, Google reviews, client referral codes, word-of-mouth chatter at events. Offer a 10% discount on the next order for leaving a review or referring a friend, and reshare client photos on official social media pages.
